Methodology

Read this before citing a number

"Real-time" means refreshed on demand

Daily bars pulled fresh from Yahoo Finance / Alpha Vantage when the pipeline runs — not live tick-by-tick streaming, which needs a paid feed.

Why pooled, not per-stock

A 100-day per-stock experiment showed AAPL's models beating their naive baseline while GOOGL's models scored below theirs — not enough data for either. Pooling ~1,000 tickers turns that into hundreds of thousands of training rows.

Direction prediction is genuinely hard

Markets are close to efficient at a 1-day horizon. Consistently beating the naive "always predict majority class" baseline, under a walk-forward split, is the real bar — not near-100% accuracy.

Risk thresholds are a starting point

Low/Medium/High cutoffs are rule-based (e.g. 30%/50% annualized volatility) for explainability, not a black box — tune and justify them against a benchmark like SPY.

Data sources, all free-tier

S&P 500 and Nifty 500 constituent lists are fetched fresh each pipeline run (not a hardcoded snapshot). Price history comes from Yahoo Finance for the full universe, Alpha Vantage for the small-watchlist mode. No paid APIs anywhere.

Every number traces back to the same tested code

The risk screen table and every stock's detail view are both computed by the same features.py / risk_metrics.py / risk_scoring.py — nothing in this dashboard is a separate reimplementation that could silently drift from the tested pipeline.
